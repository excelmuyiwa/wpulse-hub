Player setup

Follow the steps in order. It takes about 5 minutes.

  1. Download the extension

    Click the button to download the WPulse extension (a .zip file).

    Download extension

    Version: 1.2.0 · Updated: 28 Oct 2026

  2. Unzip to a folder that stays in place

    Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. Keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents. Do not move or delete it afterwards, or the extension will stop working.

    The zip also contains a file called HOW TO INSTALL.txt with the same steps.

  3. Load it into Chrome

    In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter. Then:

    1. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
    2. Click Load unpacked.
    3. Open the extracted folder, then select the inner folder called WPulse Extension (the one that contains manifest.json), not the outer folder.

    Tip: if Chrome says "Manifest file is missing", you picked the outer folder. Go one level deeper.

  4. Find your install ID

    Open Woogles in a game, then click the WPulse icon in the Chrome toolbar. Your install ID is shown in the popup. (Tip: click the puzzle-piece icon and pin WPulse if you cannot see it.)

  5. Send your install ID and wait for approval

    Send your install ID directly to the organizer. You can use any of these three contacts:

    • WhatsApp: +234 9065728333
    • Telegram: @ExcelMuyiwa
    • Discord: excelmuyiwa.

    Send these three things in one message: your install ID, your name, and your Woogles username(s).

    Then wait to be approved. The organizer will confirm when you are set.

  6. If Chrome shows a developer mode notice

    When Chrome starts, it may show a message about disabling developer mode extensions. Dismiss it. Do not remove the extension.

  7. Camera and screen-share links

How to update WPulse

When a new version is released, follow these steps.

  1. Download the new zip

    Download it from the Player setup section of this page. Check the Version line to make sure it is newer than yours.

  2. Replace the old folder

    Close your Woogles tab. Delete the old extracted folder, then extract the new zip to the same place as before (the same folder location).

  3. Refresh the extension

    Open chrome://extensions and click the refresh button (the circular arrow) on the WPulse card. Do not remove the extension.

  4. Reload Woogles

    Reload your Woogles tab.

    Your install ID stays the same, so you do not need to be approved again.

Fair play and the proctor

WPulse Duels are played under a strict no-cheating policy. Every duel is monitored by a proctor to keep things fair for both players.

Free-form duels: when a duel is announced as free-form, anyone may use engines. Otherwise, the strict no-cheating policy applies.

What the proctor does

Monitors every duel. Watches and listens to both players' screens and cameras throughout the game. Makes sure players follow the rules.

What players do

Before the duel, share your full screen, and your camera with audio, using the links the organizer sends you. The proctor uses these to watch and listen during the game.

Your screen and camera links are sent to you personally by the organizer. They are not on this page.

What is not allowed

  • No tabs or programs on your PC related to Scrabble or word checking (other than the Woogles game itself).
  • No watching or listening to the stream while you play.
  • No one may be seen next to you during a game.
  • No other device may be used to look up words or anything related to the game. A phone used as your camera is fine.
  • No messaging anyone about the game while it is in progress.
  • Switch off all notifications before the game. Notifications are monitored.
  • Keep your full screen shared and your camera on, with you in view, for the whole game.
  • In free-form duels, engines are allowed as announced. Everything else stays in force unless the duel is announced otherwise.
  • No headphones or earbuds.

When to be ready before a duel

30 minutes before a stream starts, your extension install, screen share and camera setup must all be set up and verified.

Disconnections and technical problems

If a player's connection drops, the proctor flags it and investigates.

Disputes and penalties

The proctor flags any concern during the game. The organizer makes the final decision after reviewing it. Depending on how serious the issue is, the outcome can be a warning, loss of a game, or loss of the match and disqualification from the event.

These rules may be updated as the series develops.

Your setup

During a duel, the proctor watches two feeds from you: your phone camera and your computer's full screen. Set both up using the links the organizer sends you.

What you need

  • A laptop or desktop computer with Google Chrome
  • A phone with a working front camera
  • A phone holder or tripod, and a charger
  • A stable internet connection

Phone camera

  1. Put your phone in landscape position on a stable holder or tripod, and plug it in.
  2. Place it to your side, away from your computer, so it clearly shows you, your hands and your screen.
  3. Switch on Do Not Disturb so notifications do not appear.
  4. Open your camera link on the phone. Choose the front camera and the default microphone, then start.
  5. Keep the browser tab open on the phone for the whole duel.
Screenshot or diagram: ideal phone placement

Screen share

  1. Open your screen share link on your computer.
  2. Choose to share your entire screen (not a tab or a window).
  3. Select the screen where Woogles is running. If you see a "share audio" option, tick it.
  4. Start sharing, then minimize that window. Do not close it.
Screenshot: choosing the entire screen

Test before your duel

  • Your hands and screen are clearly visible on the phone camera.
  • Your entire screen is being shared.
  • Notifications are off.
  • You have done this at least 30 minutes before the stream starts.

Headphones

Headphones and earbuds are not allowed during a duel.

Lighting and room

Play in a well-lit room, so the proctor can see you clearly. No one else may be visible during a duel.

FAQ & troubleshooting

What does the WPulse extension do?

It reads the game information that the Woogles page already shows (board, scores, clocks, moves and your own rack) and sends it to the live stream overlay. It does not play or change your game, and it never asks for your Woogles password.

What do I need to take part?

A computer with Google Chrome for Woogles and the extension, a camera with a microphone (a phone works well), and a stable internet connection. Your screen and camera links are sent to you personally by the organizer.

Why does Chrome show a "developer mode" notice?

The extension is installed from a file instead of the Chrome Web Store, so Chrome shows a notice when it starts. Dismiss it. Do not remove the extension.

The extension says "WPulse isn't connected to a Woogles game." What do I do?

Make sure you are on a game page at woogles.io, then reload the tab. If it still does not connect, message the organizer.

Do I need to be approved? How long does it take?

Yes. Send your install ID, your name and your Woogles username(s) to the organizer. Until you are approved, nothing from your install reaches the stream.

Do I need to be approved again after an update?

No. Your install ID stays the same when you update. See How to update WPulse.

Can I use an engine or word checker?

Only in duels announced as free-form. All other duels follow the strict no-cheating policy described in the Fair play and the proctor section.

All times on this page are in WAT (West Africa Time, UTC+1). Please convert to your own time zone. The schedule above lists the next duels.
