Apply to play

WPulse Gaming welcomes Scrabble players with a range of experience levels. Players are considered for matches based on the format being arranged, availability, suitability for the event and the requirements announced for that match.

To apply, message the organizer (see Contact) with:

  • your name or preferred display name
  • your Woogles.io username
  • your general availability and time zone
  • your published rating or approximate playing level, if available

A published rating is not required for every match. Whether an unrated or unrated-profile applicant is suitable will depend on the event and the opponent or format being arranged.

Submitting an application does not guarantee selection. If selected, you will receive the match details, applicable rules, technical setup instructions and camera and recording conditions. You must complete the required setup checks and receive approval before playing.

Player setup

Follow the steps in order. It takes about 5 minutes.

  1. Download the extension

    Click the button to download the WPulse extension (a .zip file).

    Download extension

    Version: 1.2.0 · Updated: 28 Oct 2026

  2. Unzip to a folder that stays in place

    Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. Keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents. Do not move or delete it afterwards, or the extension will stop working.

    The zip also contains a file called HOW TO INSTALL.txt with the same steps.

  3. Load it into Chrome

    In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter. Then:

    1. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
    2. Click Load unpacked.

    Open Woogles in a game, then click the WPulse icon in the Chrome toolbar. Your install ID is shown in the popup. (Tip: click the puzzle-piece icon and pin WPulse if you cannot see it.)

How to update WPulse

When a new version is released, follow these steps.

  1. Download the new zip

    Download it from the Player setup section of this page. Check the Version line to make sure it is newer than yours.

  2. Replace the old folder

    Close your Woogles tab. Delete the old extracted folder, then extract the new zip to the same place as before (the same folder location).

  3. Refresh the extension

    Open chrome://extensions and click the refresh button (the circular arrow) on the WPulse card. Do not remove the extension.

  4. Reload Woogles

    Reload your Woogles tab.

    Your install ID stays the same, so you do not need to be approved again.

Fair play and the proctor

WPulse Duels are monitored to help maintain fair competition. Players must follow these rules, the conditions announced for their match, and the Woogles.io Terms of Service and fair-play rules.

Standard competitive matches

Standard competitive matches are engine-free. During a game, players must not use engines, word-finding or word-lookup tools, ask anyone for move assistance, or use a live broadcast or another account to obtain help. Players must not message or call anyone for assistance about the game. Do not watch or listen to the WPulse broadcast while playing. Do not use another device to look up words or analyse the position. Keep your full computer screen shared and your camera on throughout the game. No other person may assist you or be present in the playing area unless the organizer has explicitly approved an exception in advance. Headphones and earbuds are not permitted under the standard setup rules.

Free-form and demonstration games

A match being unrated does not automatically mean engine assistance is allowed. Any engine-assisted or consultation format must be announced separately, must be nonrated, and must have the explicit agreement of both players. It must also comply with Woogles.io's rules. Unless the match announcement explicitly states otherwise, the standard engine-free rules apply. Match-specific exceptions must be agreed and announced before play begins.

What the proctor does

The proctor monitors the required camera and screen feeds, checks that players follow the announced rules, and reports technical issues or possible rule violations to the organizer. The proctor does not make the final ruling on penalties. The organizer is responsible for event decisions.

What players do

Before the duel, share your full screen, and your camera with audio, using the links the organizer sends you. The proctor uses these to watch and listen during the game.

Your screen and camera links are sent to you personally by the organizer. They are not on this page.

What is not allowed

  • No tabs or programs on your PC related to Scrabble or word checking (other than the Woogles game itself).
  • No engines, word-finding or word-lookup tools, and no asking anyone for move assistance (unless the match announcement says otherwise).
  • No watching or listening to the stream while you play.
  • No one may be seen next to you during a game.
  • No other device may be used to look up words or anything related to the game. A phone used as your camera is fine.
  • No messaging anyone about the game while it is in progress.
  • Turn off all notifications before the match. They can appear on your shared screen.
  • Keep your full screen shared and your camera on, with you in view, for the whole game.
  • No using the live broadcast or another account to get help.
  • No headphones or earbuds.

When to be ready before a duel

30 minutes before a stream starts, your extension install, screen share and camera setup must all be set up and verified.

Disconnections and technical problems

If a player loses connection or a technical problem affects the game, notify the proctor or organizer immediately. The Woogles clock may keep running. The proctor can report the issue and recommend action, but only the organizer decides how to proceed. Do not assume any interruption has been granted until the organizer confirms it. The organizer decides whether play should resume, continue, be restarted, be rescheduled, or be handled another way based on the circumstances and the applicable game rules.

Incorrect game settings

Players should verify the game settings against the match announcement before play begins. If the settings do not match, alert the organizer immediately. The organizer decides how to proceed after considering the discrepancy, when it was discovered, and whether it affected the game. Players must follow the organizer's decision.

Allegations and penalties

If a fair-play concern is raised, the organizer reviews the available evidence, gives the player an opportunity to explain where appropriate, and documents the decision. Depending on the circumstances and seriousness of the issue, outcomes may include a warning, a game or match loss, rescheduling, or disqualification from the event. An allegation alone is not proof of a violation. These are WPulse event decisions. Woogles.io retains authority over its own platform and may apply its own rules or penalties.

The rules may be updated as the series develops. Players will be informed of the rules applicable to their match before they confirm participation.

Your setup

During a duel, the proctor watches two feeds from you: your phone camera and your computer's full screen. Set both up using the links the organizer sends you.

What you need

  • A laptop or desktop computer with Google Chrome
  • A phone with a working front camera
  • A phone holder or tripod, and a charger
  • A stable internet connection

Phone camera

  1. Put your phone in landscape position on a stable holder or tripod, and plug it in.
  2. Place it to your side, away from your computer, so it clearly shows you, your hands and your screen.
  3. Switch on Do Not Disturb so notifications do not appear.
  4. Open your camera link on the phone. Choose the front camera and the default microphone, then start.
  5. Keep the browser tab open on the phone for the whole duel.
Screenshot or diagram: ideal phone placement

Screen share

  1. Open your screen share link on your computer.
  2. Choose to share your entire screen (not a tab or a window).
  3. Select the screen where Woogles is running. If you see a "share audio" option, tick it.
  4. Start sharing, then minimize that window. Do not close it.
Screenshot: choosing the entire screen

Test before your duel

  • Your hands and screen are clearly visible on the phone camera.
  • Your entire screen is being shared.
  • Notifications are off.
  • You have done this at least 30 minutes before the stream starts.

Headphones

Headphones and earbuds are not allowed during a duel.

Lighting and room

Play in a well-lit room, so the proctor can see you clearly. No one else may be visible during a duel.

Camera, screen sharing and recording

Standard competitive WPulse Duels require players to provide a working camera feed for proctoring and to agree to the announced public-broadcast arrangements before participating. Players who do not agree to the public camera requirement cannot participate in the standard competitive format. Any alternative arrangement must be approved in advance and may require a different format.

The organizer will provide each player with the relevant camera and screen-sharing links. The proctor uses the required feeds to monitor the match. Selected player camera footage may also appear in the public broadcast, replay or other WPulse match content. Screen feeds are used for proctoring only and are not shown in the public broadcast.

WPulse matches are recorded for broadcast production and may be reviewed when a technical issue, complaint or fair-play concern needs to be assessed. Before confirming participation, players will be told how their feeds and recordings are intended to be used.

Published footage and removal requests

Published match footage is normally retained as part of WPulse's match and broadcast archive. Players may contact the organizer to request removal, restriction or editing of particular footage. Requests will be reviewed individually. WPulse may make an exception where there is a compelling privacy or safety concern, a legal requirement, a significant error, or another exceptional reason under the event's policy. A request does not guarantee removal, and the organizer will consider the circumstances before deciding. Raw recordings and published videos are treated separately. Removing or restricting a public video does not necessarily mean every underlying recording or piece of evidence will be deleted immediately. By accepting a match invitation, players confirm that they understand and agree to the announced camera, broadcast and recording conditions. Contact the organizer before accepting if you have a question or concern.

FAQ & troubleshooting

What does the WPulse extension do?

It reads the game information that the Woogles page already shows (board, scores, clocks, moves and your own rack) plus your Woogles username, and sends it to the live stream overlay. It does not play or change your game, and it never asks for your Woogles password.

What do I need to take part?

A computer with Google Chrome for Woogles and the extension, a camera with a microphone (a phone works well), and a stable internet connection. Your screen and camera links are sent to you personally by the organizer.

Why does Chrome show a "developer mode" notice?

The extension is installed from a file instead of the Chrome Web Store, so Chrome shows a notice when it starts. Dismiss it. Do not remove the extension.

The extension says "WPulse isn't connected to a Woogles game." What do I do?

Make sure you are on a game page at woogles.io, then reload the tab. If it still does not connect, message the organizer.

Do I need to be approved? How long does it take?

Yes. Send your install ID, your name and your Woogles username(s) to the organizer. Until you are approved, nothing from your install reaches the stream.

Do I need to be approved again after an update?

No. Your install ID stays the same when you update. See How to update WPulse.

Can I use an engine or word checker?

Not in standard matches. Engine-assisted or demonstration formats are announced separately, must be nonrated, and need both players' explicit agreement. See Fair play and the proctor.

What time will duels be, and in which time zone?

All times on this page are in WAT (West Africa Time, UTC+1). Please convert to your own time zone. The schedule above lists the next duels.

Who can see my screen and camera?

The proctor and organizer watch your camera and screen feeds during a duel. Your camera footage may also appear in the public broadcast, replay or other WPulse match content. Your screen is used for proctoring only and is never shown in the public broadcast. See Camera, screen sharing and recording.

Can I play if I do not have a published rating?

Possibly. It depends on the match being arranged. Include your approximate playing level and experience when applying if you do not have a published rating.

Can I decline the public camera requirement?

The public camera requirement applies to the standard competitive format. If you cannot agree to it, you cannot participate in that format. Contact the organizer before accepting an invitation to ask whether another format is available.

Can I ask for a published video to be removed?

Yes. Contact the organizer and explain your concern. Published match footage is normally retained, but exceptional requests may be considered under WPulse's footage policy. Removal is not guaranteed.

Who can authorize a stop or restart if a player disconnects?

Only the organizer decides how to proceed. Tell the proctor or organizer as soon as a problem occurs, and wait for the organizer's instructions.

What if the Woogles settings are different from the match announcement?

Report the mismatch immediately. The organizer decides whether the game should proceed, be corrected, restarted or handled another way. Do not assume the game is approved to continue until the organizer confirms it.

What happens if someone is accused of cheating?

The organizer reviews the available evidence, hears the player's explanation where appropriate, and documents the decision. Any action depends on the evidence and circumstances. Woogles.io may also apply its own platform rules.

Still stuck? Contact the organizer using the details in the Contact section.
